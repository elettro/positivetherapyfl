The Science of Sustainable Weight Loss

Our approach is rooted in clinical research, targeting the biological mechanisms that govern weight.

Metabolic Health

We focus on optimizing your metabolic rate and improving insulin sensitivity, ensuring your body efficiently processes energy rather than storing it as fat.

Hormonal Balance

GLP-1 medications mimic natural hormones to regulate hunger signals in the brain, helping you feel full sooner and reducing persistent food noise.

Inflammation Reduction

Chronic inflammation is a key driver of weight gain. Our protocols help lower systemic inflammation, supporting overall cellular health and longevity.

Top 10 Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is GLP-1 and how does it work? expand_more

GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1) is a hormone that occurs naturally in the body. Medications like Semaglutide mimic this hormone to slow stomach emptying and signal fullness to the brain.

Q2: Is this program right for me? expand_more

Our program is designed for adults with a BMI over 30, or over 27 with weight-related health conditions, who are looking for a medically-supervised approach.

Q3: What medications do you prescribe? expand_more

We primarily prescribe FDA-approved GLP-1 medications such as Semaglutide and Tirzepatide, tailored to your specific health profile.

Q4: Do I need lab work? expand_more

Yes, recent lab work is required to ensure the medication is safe for you and to establish a baseline for your metabolic health.

Q5: How quickly will I see results? expand_more

While individual results vary, many patients begin to see weight changes within the first 4 weeks as the medication reaches therapeutic levels.

Q6: Are there side effects? expand_more

Common side effects include mild nausea or digestive changes. We provide guidance and titration schedules to minimize these effects.

Q7: Is insurance accepted for GLP-1 medications? expand_more

Coverage varies by plan. We can provide documentation for you to submit to your insurance, or discuss affordable out-of-pocket options.

Q8: How often are the consultations? expand_more

We typically schedule monthly check-ins to monitor your progress, adjust dosages, and provide ongoing support.

Q9: What happens after I reach my goal weight? expand_more

We transition you to a maintenance phase focused on lifestyle sustainability and gradual medication tapering if appropriate.

Q10: How do I get started? expand_more

Simply click the 'Book Consultation' button to schedule your initial assessment with our medical team.
