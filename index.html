Common Questions

Everything you need to know about starting therapy.

Do you accept insurance? expand_more
We are an out-of-network provider for most insurance plans. This allows us to provide highly personalized care without the restrictions often imposed by insurance companies. We can provide superbills for you to submit for potential out-of-network reimbursement.
How does online therapy work? expand_more
Online therapy sessions are conducted via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. You simply need a quiet, private space and a reliable internet connection. It offers the exact same level of care as in-person therapy, with added flexibility.
