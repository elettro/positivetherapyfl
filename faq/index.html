person Individual Counseling

Your first session, often called an intake, is primarily about getting to know you. We will discuss your background, the reasons you are seeking therapy, and your goals for treatment. It's a collaborative conversation to ensure we are a good fit and to begin drafting a tailored treatment plan. You are not expected to have all the answers right away; we will navigate the process together at your pace.

The duration of therapy is highly individualized. Some clients find resolution to specific, acute issues in 8-12 weeks using focused modalities. Others engage in longer-term therapy to explore deeper patterns, complex trauma, or ongoing personal growth over several months or years. We regularly review your progress to ensure the therapy remains effective and aligned with your evolving goals.

favorite Couples Counseling

Generally, yes. Couples therapy is most effective when both partners are actively engaged in the process together. However, your therapist may occasionally recommend an individual session with each partner as part of the initial assessment phase to better understand individual perspectives. The primary work, however, is done jointly.

psychology EMDR Therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is an evidence-based therapy designed to alleviate the distress associated with traumatic memories. It uses bilateral stimulation (like eye movements or tapping) while you focus on a distressing memory. This process helps the brain reprocess the memory, reducing its emotional charge and allowing you to develop more adaptive coping mechanisms.

videocam Telemental Health

Extensive research indicates that teletherapy is generally just as effective as in-person therapy for most mental health concerns, including anxiety, depression, and trauma. It offers the added benefits of convenience, accessibility, and the comfort of participating from your own space. We use a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform to ensure your privacy.
