How It Works

Beginning therapy shouldn't be stressful. We've created a seamless, supportive process from your first click to your first session.

1

Request an Appointment

Use our secure online portal to select a time that works for you. You'll answer a few brief questions to help us match you with the right approach.

2

Intake & Assessment

Before we meet, you'll complete a confidential intake form. In our first session, we'll discuss your goals and map out a collaborative plan.

3

Ongoing Support

Meet regularly in our calming space or via telehealth. We'll continuously evaluate your progress and adjust our approach to ensure you're moving forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about starting individual therapy.

How long is a typical counseling session? expand_more
Standard individual counseling sessions are 50 minutes long. This allows ample time to explore core issues while leaving 10 minutes for your therapist to finalize clinical notes and prepare for the next steps in your care plan.
What can I expect in the first session? expand_more
The first session is primarily an assessment and get-to-know-you period. We'll review your intake paperwork, discuss what brought you to therapy, and explore your personal history to establish a foundation for our work together.
Do you offer in-person or virtual sessions? expand_more
We offer both. You can choose to meet in our tranquil coastal office environment, or opt for secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth sessions from the comfort of your own home.

Your journey toward better mental health begins with a single conversation. Schedule your individual session today and start feeling grounded again.
