Common Questions

We understand that seeking support can bring up questions.

What is trauma-informed therapy? add

It means we assume that past experiences influence current struggles. We prioritize creating a safe environment, establishing trustworthiness, and empowering you, rather than just asking "what's wrong with you."

Can I recover from years of trauma? add

Yes. While the events themselves cannot be erased, the distress associated with them can be significantly reduced. Healing is a process of rewiring the nervous system to feel safe in the present.

What if I don't want to talk about the details? add

You never have to share details until or unless you feel completely ready. Many somatic and grounding techniques allow us to process the physical impacts of trauma without requiring a narrative retelling of the events.
