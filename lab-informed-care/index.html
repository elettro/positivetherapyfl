science Integrative Approach

Lab-Informed Care for Deeper Healing.

Mental health isn't just in the mind—it's deeply connected to your biology. We utilize advanced diagnostic testing to uncover hidden physical factors, creating a truly personalized roadmap to your well-being.

Why We Look Beneath the Surface

Traditional therapy addresses the emotional and psychological. Lab-informed care adds the biological piece, ensuring we aren't fighting an uphill battle against your own physiology.

water_drop

Hormonal Balance

Fluctuations in thyroid, cortisol, and reproductive hormones can profoundly mimic or exacerbate symptoms of anxiety and depression.

monitor_heart

Metabolic & Nutritional Deficiencies

Your brain requires specific nutrients to produce neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine. We test for critical deficiencies in Vitamin D, B12, iron, and key amino acids that directly impact mood regulation and cognitive clarity.

local_fire_department

Inflammation Markers

Chronic inflammation isn't just a physical issue; neuroinflammation is strongly linked to treatment-resistant depression, brain fog, and chronic fatigue. Identifying these markers allows for targeted lifestyle interventions.

psychology

Genetic Insights

Understanding your genetic predispositions (like MTHFR mutations) helps us tailor supplemental support for optimal brain health.

The Pathway to Clarity

A seamless integration of clinical testing and therapeutic care.

1

Comprehensive Testing

We order a customized panel of blood work and metabolic tests based on your specific symptoms and history, conveniently drawn at a local lab.

2

In-Depth Analysis

Our clinical team reviews your results, looking not just for "normal" ranges, but for optimal functional levels that support mental well-being.

3

Personalized Care Plan

We integrate findings into your therapy plan, combining psychotherapeutic techniques with evidence-based nutritional and lifestyle recommendations.
